# 2026-11-21 — UI update 1: see-through scanner, light mode, calculator

**Milestone:** v0.3 · **Branch:** `ui-update-1` · **Tests:** 128 unit tests + browser runs of 19, 34, 28 and 10 checks

## Goal
Four changes from using the app on real cards: a bottom panel you can see the camera through (like Phynite), a scanner that looks less blocky, light and dark mode, and a plain calculator next to the offer calculator.

## What I built
- **Full-screen camera.** The camera moved behind everything; the header and bottom panel are frosted glass on top. The scan frame is placed in the visible gap between them, and the photo crop still maps screen to camera pixels exactly (checked: the saved photo is card-shaped, 0.714 ≈ 63:88).
- **Softer frame.** No dark mask, thin rounded corners with a soft shadow so they show on bright tables, a glass status chip, and a quick squeeze on tap.
- **Themes.** Every hard-coded colour became a variable, with a light set alongside the dark one. A few lines in `index.html` apply the saved theme before the stylesheet loads, so there's no dark flash in light mode.
- **Calculator.** `calc.js` works out the expression by hand (no `eval`), with × and ÷ before + and −, and % the way people price things (200 + 10% = 220; 200 × 15% = 30). The sheet can drop in the bucket's market value, offer and paid amount.

## What broke / what I learned
- **Yellow text disappears on white.** The accent colour works as a fill in both themes but not as text, so text now uses a separate darker gold in light mode.
- **A full-screen preview zooms in.** On a tall screen the camera is cropped at the sides, so the same card looks bigger. My fake test camera was sized for the old layout and its card overflowed the frame, so the text reader missed the number. I resized the test card; on a real phone you just hold the card a little farther away.
- **Toasts sit under open dialogs.** The calculator's "Copied" message shows inside the calculator instead.
